# Gluten free crispy chicken pancake

## Ingredients
* 200g chicken breast, diced

* 5 tbsp potato starch

* 1 tbsp water

* 2 scallions, chopped

* 1/2 tsp garlic powder

* 1/2 tsp onion powder

* 1/2 tsp white pepper

* Pinch of salt

* Oil, for frying

## Topping
Traditionally, this is topped with scallions, but I wanted something a little more refreshing, so I went with shredded cucumber instead.

## Dipping sauce
Mix together soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, and chili oil.

## Instructions
1. Season the chicken with salt, white pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.

2. Add the [potato starch and water, mixing until everything is evenly coated.

3. Pour the mixture into a hot, oiled pan and spread into an even layer.

4. Fry until deeply golden and crispy on both sides.

5. Top wiht a generous handful of scallions or cucumber.

6. Serve with the dipping sauce and enjoy!

Crunchy on the outside, juicy on the inside, and packed with flavor.
