# Miso scrambled egg

## Ingredients
4 large eggs
1 tsp white sugar
2 tsp white miso paste
2 tsp water
2 tsp toasted sesame oil
furikake (to taste)
1 scallion (chopped)
shichimi tagarashi (to taste)
roasted laver (cut into strips)
black sesame seeds (to taste)

## Instructions
1. Crack the eggs into a medium mixing bowl, add the sugar, and whisk until smooth.

2. In a small bowl, stir together the miso paste and water until combined, then pour it into the egg mixture and whisk to combine.

3. In a pan over low heat, add the sesame oil and then the egg mixture.

4. Cook as usual until the eggs are just barely set, then pull the pan off the heat.

5. Plate the eggs over steamed rice topped with furikake, then finish with a small sprinkle of shichimi togarashi and bolack sesame seeds, as well as some chopped scallions. For even more flavour, cut some pieces of roasted laver into thin strips and place them on top of the eggs.
