# Mini chicken pancakes (Padak-jeon)

## Ingredients

### Chicken marinade
* 2 lbs chicken breast (chopped fine)
* 2 tsp salt
* pepper
* garlic powder
* 1 tbsp mirin

Rest 30 minutes.

### Pancake batter

Make constarch slurry (4 tbsp cornstarch + 4 tbsp water) then mix in marinated chicken and chopped green onions.

### Dipping sauce

2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp mirin, 1/2 tbsp garlic, and sesame seeds.

## Instructions
Pan-fry mini patties in oil until super crispy!
