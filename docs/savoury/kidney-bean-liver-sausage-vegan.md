# Kidney bean smoked tofu liver sausage vegan

=== "Variant 1"
    <!-- Taken from: [cheapandcheerfulcooking](https://cheapandcheerfulcooking.com) -->
    ## Ingredients
    1 can kidney beans, 240g drained weight

    150g smoked tofu

    1 onion, small

    2 tsp marjoram

    1 tsp mustard

    1 tsp smoked salt, alternatively regular salt

    1/2 tsp pepper

    2 tbsp vegetable oil, e.g. rapeseed oil

    ## Preparation
    Finely dice the onion and saut'e in a pan with a dash of vegetable oil at medium temperature for  a few minutes. Add marjoram and sweat briefly. Remove from the heat and let cool down a bit.

    Drain the beans well. Roughly crumble the tofu. Mix with all the other ingredients in a food processor or with the help of a hand blender until the vegan liver sausage has the desired consistency. If necessary, add a few drops of water in the course of this.

=== "Variant 2"

    ## Ingredients
    100g smoked tofu

    125g kidney beans (tin)

    1/2 red onion

    1 tbsp olive oil

    1 tbsp parsley

    1 tsp marjoram

    1/2 tsp salt

    pepper

    ## Instructions
    Rinse and drain the kidney beans. Finely dice the onion and coarsely dice the smoked tofu.

    Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the onions until translucent. Add the marjoram and fry briefly until fragrant.

    Place the tofu, kidney beans, onions, parsley, salt, and pepper into the food processor and blend for 1–2 minutes until the desired consistency is reached.
