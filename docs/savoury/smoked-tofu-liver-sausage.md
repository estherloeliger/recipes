# Smoked tofu liver sausage

## Ingredients

| Key | Value |
|-----|-------|
|100g |smoked tofu |
|125g |kidney beans (tin) |
|1/2 | red onion |
|1 tablespoon | olive oil |
|1 tablespoon | parsley |
|1 teaspoon heaped | marjoram |
|1/2 tablespoon|salt |
| | pepper |

## Preparation
Wash kidney beans and let them dry. Cube onion (fine) and smoked tofu (coarse).

Warm oil in a pan and add onion until transparent. Add marjoram and fry briefly until fragrant.

Blend tofu, kidney beans, onion, parsley, salt and pepper for 1-2 minutes to desired consistency.

## Notes
If you like liver sausage creamy, add 1 tablespoon soya or almond cuisine.
